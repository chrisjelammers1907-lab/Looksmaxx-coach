# CHARETT public demo

This branch serves the self-contained CHARETT website from its root.
Home is For You/Following; Categories contains eight tools and beginner guides.
AI Coach, My Plan and Messages are available in navigation; About us is public.
Photo and video posts can attach an original demo instrumental sound.

Only fictional content, licensed stock and clearly labelled original AI editorial
inspiration are embedded. No owner photos, routines, PDFs, brief or credentials
are included. AI coach, shopping, social activity, accounts and messaging remain
labelled simulations. The three sounds are original instrumentals, not a
commercial music catalogue. Personal uploads remain in browser-tab memory.

Source and documentation: demo/charett-preview branch.
The Verify CHARETT publication workflow checks the exact public HTML and Chrome
photo/video sound, navigation and social journeys without a login, then records
results on charett-publication-status.
